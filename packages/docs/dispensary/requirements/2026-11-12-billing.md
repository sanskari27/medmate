# Billing

## Context

An approved pharmacy posts a cash sale from one counter screen. The screen does not copy the pharmacy app. Search, scan, and discount math run on inventory and offers already loaded into Redux. The pharmacy app stays untouched and is not imported. The API is [Pharmacy billing](../../medmate-server/requirements/2026-11-12-pharmacy-billing.md).

## Scope

- A Counter item, Billing, for an approved owner and for pharmacy staff who hold `billing:read`.
- `/billing`, the first sidebar item, above Dashboard.
- A scan field, a result table, a cart table, patient fields, cash as the only pay mode, and Charge.
- The Manage Users dialog gains the Billing module under Counter, before Management.

## Non-goals

- A bill list, reprint from a list, returns, loose units, and UPI or card capture.
- A manual product-card grid.
- The admin portal and the pharmacy app.

## Behavior

The first nav item is Counter / Billing. The group label Counter is on that item. Dashboard follows, then Catalogue, then Management. An approved owner always sees Billing. A staff member sees it only with `billing:read`. Charge alone leaves the item hidden, and `/billing` sends that person to the dashboard.

The shell title is Billing and the subtitle is `Cash counter`. Opening the page loads `GET /pharmacy/inventory` and `GET /pharmacy/offers` through the portal API client into the Redux store. Later keystrokes do not fetch. The cart, the patient, and the posted bill live in a billing slice.

The counter is one column so a sale can be finished from the keyboard: search, then the line grid, then a settlement dock. The scan field is focused on open. Enter in that field adds a product and never charges. After a line is added, its quantity is focused and the digits already there are selected once, so the next digits append. Shift+Enter, Esc, and F3 return to search. Matches open in a list over the grid, so the grid does not move, and only while the query is non-empty, at most eight rows. Each row shows the name, a truncated composition, the product id, the sellable quantity, and the MRP hint. Search is case-insensitive on name, composition, and product id. An exact product id wins over a name that merely contains that text. The first row is highlighted. Up and Down move the highlight. Enter adds the highlighted row, or the exact product id when one matches. A row with no sellable batch is visible and cannot be added. Sellable means quantity above zero and an expiry month on or after the current month in Asia/Kolkata. The MRP hint is the earliest sellable batch.

The grid is compact. Columns are #, Product, Batch, Qty, MRP, Offer, Special, CGST, SGST, GST, Disc, and Amt. A new line has quantity 1 and the earliest sellable batch. The batch menu lists sellable batches with batch, expiry, quantity, and MRP. The same product and the same batch are one line. Another scan increments that line and cannot pass the batch quantity. A second batch is a second line. Product text shows the name and the product id. Search shows only the first part of the composition. Offer is the winning offer and the rupees it takes off. Special is an extra discount on that row: none, a percent, or rupees, applied to the unit price after the offer, and it cannot take that unit below zero. GST is the tax included in the line amount. CGST and SGST are each half of that GST, with the extra paisa on CGST so the two halves add up. Disc is the offer plus the special discount. Amt is the line total. Tab moves to the next cell. In quantity, Enter moves to the next cell, + and − step the quantity, and Up and Down move between quantity cells. Alt+Backspace removes the focused row. F2 focuses that row's special discount. F3 focuses search. F4 focuses the phone. / focuses search when the cashier is not already typing.

The dock is the customer, then settlement, then a bill summary and Charge. The summary shows item and unit counts, gross MRP, offer discount, row special, bill discount, total discount, taxable value, CGST, SGST, GST included, the amount saved, cash received, change, and balance due. Patient name and a ten-digit phone are always shown. Prescribed by and Prescription number are shown when any cart line has `prescriptionRequired`. Cash is selected. UPI and Card are visible and disabled. Cash received and the return change are shown for the cashier and are not sent. Subtotal, offers, extras, GST, and payable follow the server rules: one winning active offer per line, a flat amount capped at the unit MRP, a percent of MRP, product scope beating an all-products offer on a tie, then the higher offer number. The bill extra has the same three choices, applied after the line extras and capped at the amount still due. Charge is hidden without `billing:create`. Charge stays disabled until every line has a batch, every quantity fits that batch, the patient is valid, and a prescription line has both prescription fields. Ctrl+Enter charges when Charge is enabled.

Charge sends `POST /pharmacy/bills` with the patient, `payMode` `cash`, and lines of product id, batch, and quantity. It does not send prices. A failure shows the problem detail beside Charge. Success opens the invoice from Invoice Settings in a modal over the counter. Esc or New bill closes it. Print prints that invoice. New bill clears the cart and the patient and focuses the scan field. The settings preview is unchanged when no bill is passed in.

The permission dialog lists Counter before Management. Billing has Read and Charge. The keys are `billing:read` and `billing:create`. The access count is `n / 33`.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner sees Billing first, under Counter, then Dashboard. A staff member with `billing:read` sees Billing. A staff member with only `billing:create` who opens `/billing` lands on the dashboard.
- A product-id scan adds a line. The result table matches a composition. UPI and Card are disabled. Charge is absent without `billing:create`.
- An active offer is shown on the matching line before Charge. After a successful post, the invoice shows the bill number. New bill clears it.
- The Manage Users dialog includes Billing Read and Charge. The access count uses 33.
