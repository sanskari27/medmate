# Expenses

## Context

An approved pharmacy lists what it has spent and records another expense. The screen follows the pharmacy app's Expenses layout. The pharmacy app stays untouched and is not imported. The API is [Pharmacy expenses](../../medmate-server/requirements/2026-11-13-pharmacy-expenses.md).

## Scope

- A Business item, Expenses, immediately after Catalogue and before Management, for an approved owner and for pharmacy staff who hold `expenses:read`.
- `/expenses`.
- The date range, category filter, search, four summary cards, paged table, Create Expense dialog, and Excel and PDF of the filtered register.
- The Manage Users dialog gains the Expenses module under a Business area, after Catalogue.

## Non-goals

- A Reports item, and the pharmacy Reports menu that opens Expense Transactions or Expense Category.
- Editing or deleting a saved expense.
- A separate export permission. Excel and PDF are available to anyone who can open the page.
- The admin portal, the pharmacy app, and unrelated dispensary screens.

## Behavior

Expenses sits under Business, immediately after Offers and before Employees. The first visible Business item carries the heading Business. An approved owner always sees Expenses. A staff member sees it only with `expenses:read`. Create alone leaves the item hidden, and `/expenses` sends that person to the dashboard.

The shell title is Expenses and the subtitle is `Track business spend & categories`. The nav icon is a cash glyph added to the dispensary icons. The page loads `GET /pharmacy/expenses` through the portal API client with `page`, `size` 25, `from`, `to`, `category` when it is not all, and `q` when the search is not blank.

The toolbar follows the pharmacy order. The date range defaults to Last 365 Days. Its presets are Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, Last 7 Days, This Month, Previous Month, Last 30 Days, This Quarter, Previous Quarter, Current Fiscal Year, Previous Fiscal Year, Last 365 Days, and Custom. Weeks start on Monday. A quarter is a calendar quarter. A fiscal year runs 1 April through 31 March. Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, and Last 365 Days end today and include that many Asia/Kolkata dates. Custom uses the two dates the person picks. The category select starts with All expense categories and then lists `categories` from the response. Search placeholder is `Search expense, party…`. Search waits 300ms, then sends `q` and returns to page 1. A new range or category also returns to page 1. Create Expense is shown only with `expenses:create`.

Four cards follow, in order: Expenses (`summary.count`), Total spend (`summary.total`), GST (ITC eligible) (`summary.gst`), and Period. Period shows the preset name. Custom shows the from and to dates. The cards follow the filter, so they stay put when only the page changes.

The columns are Date, Expense No., Party Name, Category, Payment, and Amount. The date is `spentOn`. Payment is Cash, UPI, Card, or Cash on Delivery. An empty filter shows `No expenses match the current filter.` When `summary.count` is above zero, the table footer reads `Total · {count} expenses` and shows `summary.total`, including rows that are not on this page. The shared page bar sits under the table. Previous and next follow the existing page-bar rules. The label is `page N of M`.

When `summary.count` is above zero, a row under the table reads `Download the filtered expense register` and offers Excel and PDF. Excel downloads a CSV. Both files use `GET /pharmacy/expenses/export` with the current `from`, `to`, `category`, and `q`, and include every matching expense. The columns are Expense no, Date, Paid to, Category, Mode, and Amount, plus a TOTAL row of the amounts. The files are built in the browser.

Create Expense opens a dialog. The fields, in order, are Date and Paid to, then Category and Payment mode, then a text field `Or a new category` under the category, then Amount ₹ (incl. GST) and GST %, then Note. GST % is 0, 5, 12, 18, or 28. Date cannot be after today in Asia/Kolkata. A non-blank custom category is the category that is saved. Save stays disabled when the amount is not positive or Paid to is blank. Save sends `POST /pharmacy/expenses`. A failure shows the problem detail. Success closes the dialog and reloads the list. When GST is above zero and the amount is positive, the dialog shows the taxable value and the GST amount before save.

The permission dialog lists Business after Catalogue. Expenses has Read and Create. The keys are `expenses:read` and `expenses:create`. The access count is `n / 39`.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner sees Offers, then Expenses under Business, then Employees under Management. A staff member with `expenses:read` sees Expenses. A staff member with only `expenses:create` who opens `/expenses` lands on the dashboard.
- The four cards use `summary`. The page bar requests `size` 25. Create Expense is absent without `expenses:create`. Excel requests the export URL and does not send `page`.
- An empty filter shows `No expenses match the current filter.` A saved expense closes the dialog. The Manage Users dialog includes Expenses Read and Expenses Create. The access count uses 39.
