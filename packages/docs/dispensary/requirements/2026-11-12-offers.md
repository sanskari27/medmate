# Offers

## Context

An approved pharmacy keeps the discounts it will later apply at billing. The dispensary screen copies the pharmacy Offers page: the heading, the create dialog, and the offer cards. It adds edit and delete, and it leaves out shopper categories and dates. The pharmacy app stays untouched and is not imported. The API is [Pharmacy offers](../../medmate-server/requirements/2026-11-12-pharmacy-offers.md).

## Scope

- A Catalogue item, Offers, for an approved owner and for pharmacy staff who hold `offers:read`.
- `/offers`.
- Creating an offer, editing its fields, pausing or resuming it, and deleting it.
- The Manage Users dialog gains the Offers module under Catalogue, after Inventory.

## Non-goals

- Applying a discount to MRP, a bill, or the customer app.
- Shopper categories, start and end dates, and the online-store direct offer.
- Choosing which overlapping offer wins. A later billing module does that.
- The admin portal and the pharmacy app.

## Behavior

The nav order under Catalogue is Distributors, Purchases, Inventory, Offers. The first visible Catalogue item carries the heading. An approved owner always sees Offers. A staff member sees it only with `offers:read`. Create, Update, or Delete alone leave the item hidden, and `/offers` sends that person to the dashboard.

The shell title is Offers and the subtitle is `Discounts & promotions`. The page loads `GET /pharmacy/offers` through the portal API client. The heading is Active & scheduled offers, with Create offer on the right. `offers:create` shows that button.

An empty list shows the tag mark, “No offers yet”, and “Create a discount to attract online customers.” Otherwise the cards sit in a wrapping grid. A card shows the gold offer mark, the pause switch, the title, a percent or rupee pill, the coupon code, “Applies to all products” or “Applies to 1 product”, the item count, and Running or Paused. A paused card stays faded. The footer keeps the status on the left. Edit and Delete sit on the right.

The pause switch, Edit, and Save offer require `offers:update`. Delete requires `offers:delete`. A missing key hides that control. The switch sends `active` only. Edit opens the same dialog, titled “Edit offer”, with the fields filled and the button “Save offer”. Delete asks “Delete this offer?” with the title, then Cancel or Delete.

The dialog fields, in order, are title, coupon code, discount type, percent or amount, Applies to, and the medicine select when the scope is one product. The coupon is uppercased as typed. Discount type is Percentage % or Flat ₹. Applies to is All products or One product. The medicine list is the `products` array on the offers response. Launch offer and Save offer stay disabled until the title, code, and a value above zero with at most two decimal places are present, and a medicine is selected when the scope is one product. A new offer starts running. A failed save shows the problem detail on the dialog.

The permission dialog lists Offers after Inventory. Offers has Read, Create, Update, and Delete. The keys are `offers:read`, `offers:create`, `offers:update`, and `offers:delete`. The access count is `n / 27`.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner sees Catalogue as Distributors, Purchases, Inventory, and Offers. A staff member with `offers:read` sees Offers. A staff member with only `offers:create` who opens `/offers` lands on the dashboard.
- The empty list shows “No offers yet”. A percent offer shows its code and “all products”. A single-medicine offer shows “1 product”.
- Create offer is absent without `offers:create`. The pause switch and Edit are absent without `offers:update`. Delete is absent without `offers:delete`.
- The Manage Users dialog includes Offers Read, Create, Update, and Delete. The access count uses 27.
