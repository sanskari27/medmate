# Profile

## Context

The dispensary shell is the pharmacy owner's console. Profile sits in Management after Manage Users and before Help & Support. The screen follows pharmacy Business Settings: the same element order, without the Online store toggle, the Distributors block, or Store setup wizard. The pharmacy app stays untouched and is not imported. The API is [Pharmacy profile](../../medmate-server/requirements/2026-11-12-pharmacy-profile.md).

## Scope

- A Profile item under Management for an approved pharmacy owner.
- `/profile`.
- Editing the stored pharmacy profile, including licence and pharmacist files.
- A confirmation before every save.
- A warning, then the existing application-status screen, when a save starts a new compliance review.

## Non-goals

- The existing Settings item. It stays where it is.
- Online store, Distributors, and Store setup wizard.
- Editing pharmacy type, invoice prefix, Accept online orders, self-order kiosk, loose selling, or low-stock alerts. Pharmacy type is shown and disabled. The other stored values stay off this screen.
- A GST registered yes/no control.
- Changing the login phone.
- Pharmacy staff access.
- The admin portal, the pharmacy app, and the setup wizard screen.
- A toast.

## Behavior

The nav item is visible only when `pharmacyRole` is `owner`. Manage Users is the first Management item. Profile follows it, before Help & Support. Pharmacy staff have no item. `/profile` sends staff to `/dashboard`.

The shell title is Profile. The subtitle is `Edit your pharmacy details — shown on invoices & reports`.

The page loads `GET /pharmacy/application` through the portal API client. Business is one full-width card, then classification, then licences. Invoice sits beside extra business details. Save and Discard are centered at the bottom of the form.

The business card is logo, business name, tagline, company phone, company email, billing address, state, pincode, and city. Phone shows the account phone, is read-only, and the helper is `Login uses this number, so it cannot be changed.` Logo can be replaced or removed.

Licences are GSTIN, PAN, drug licence, FSSAI, and trade licence. Each number sits beside its file row. Then Enable e-Invoicing, Enable TDS, Enable TCS, the registered pharmacist, and the pharmacist certificate. A licence file or the pharmacist certificate can be replaced and cannot be removed without a new file.

Classification is pharmacy type, industry, registration type, and the business types. Pharmacy type is disabled. The helper is `Set during setup, so it cannot be changed.` Terms and signature follow. Signature can be replaced and cannot be cleared. The note on that card says terms and signature are shown on invoices.

Extra details are label and value rows, such as MSME or a website. Empty is allowed.

Save and Discard stay disabled until something changed. Discard restores the loaded profile. Save always opens a dialog titled `Save changes?` with Cancel and Save changes.

When no review field changed, the body is `Your pharmacy details will be saved.` A successful save stays on `/profile` and clears the dirty state. If the business name changed, the sidebar name updates.

Review fields are GSTIN, PAN, drug licence number, FSSAI number, trade licence number, and their files, plus the pharmacist name, pharmacist certificate, and signature. When any of those changed, the body is `Licence, pharmacist, or signature changes are sent for review. You will not be able to use the console until the pharmacy is approved again.`

One save writes the whole form. A review save sets the session to `pending_compliance`. The existing home path then opens `/application-status`. A later rejection still uses `/setup`. Profile is only available while the pharmacy is approved. There is no second draft and no toast.

A stored file that the owner does not replace is omitted from the multipart body. A newly chosen image is previewed. A PDF, and a stored file that was not replaced, is shown as Attached.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner sees Management, Help & Support, and Profile. Staff do not, and staff opening `/profile` land on the dashboard.
- Phone is read-only. Pharmacy type is disabled. Online store, Distributors, and Store setup wizard are absent.
- A tagline save asks for confirmation, stays on Profile, and leaves the pharmacy approved.
- A GSTIN save shows the lockout warning. After confirm, the owner leaves the console for the application-status path.
