# Sales

## Context

An approved pharmacy lists the cash invoices it has posted and can return units from one of them. The screen does not copy the pharmacy app. The pharmacy app stays untouched and is not imported. The API is [Pharmacy sales](../../medmate-server/requirements/2026-11-13-pharmacy-sales.md).

## Scope

- A Counter item, Sales, after Billing, for an approved owner and for pharmacy staff who hold `sales:read`.
- `/sales` and `/sales/:id`.
- Three month cards, the invoice list, the existing invoice paper, and a partial return.
- The Manage Users dialog gains the Sales module under Counter, after Billing.

## Non-goals

- A date filter, paging, CSV, PDF, share, channel, and payment status. Search stays on the loaded list and does not change the API.
- Editing or deleting a saved invoice or return.
- A second GST invoice for a return, and a credit-note print.
- The admin portal, the pharmacy app, and the Billing counter.

## Behavior

Sales sits under Counter, immediately after Billing. The first visible Counter item carries the heading Counter. Dashboard follows. An approved owner always sees Sales. A staff member sees it only with `sales:read`. Return alone leaves the item hidden, and `/sales` sends that person to the dashboard.

The shell title is Sales and the subtitle is `Every sale, day one to date`. The page loads `GET /pharmacy/sales` through the portal API client. Opening a row loads `GET /pharmacy/sales/{id}`. Invoice paper also loads `GET /pharmacy/invoice-settings`.

Three cards follow, in order: Sales this month (amount, hint is the invoice count), Returns this month (amount, hint is the return count), and Net this month (the signed amount, hint is `Sales minus returns`). The heading Billed invoices sits above the table, with a search field on the same row (`Search invoice or patient…`). Search matches the document id, invoice number, patient name, and type label on the loaded list, ignoring case. The month cards stay the full totals. A query with no match shows `No sales match your search.` An empty list shows `No sales yet.` The columns are Type, Invoice no., Patient, Date, Items, and Amount. Amounts stay positive. The type label is Sale or Return. The date is the Asia/Kolkata calendar date of `recordedAt`. A row opens `/sales/:id`.

An invoice opens the invoice paper from Invoice Settings in a dialog. Print prints that paper. Esc or Close returns to the list. `sales:return` shows Return while any unit remains. The return form lists each line with the sold quantity, the quantity already returned, and a return quantity. The preview uses the same refund rule as the server: the bill extra is spread by line net, and the earliest units of a line take the extra paisa. Save stays disabled when every quantity is zero or any quantity is not a whole number within the remaining units. Save sends `POST /pharmacy/sales/{invoiceNumber}/returns` with position and quantity only. A failure shows the problem detail. Success returns to the list.

A return row opens a summary of the patient, the original invoice number, the lines, and the amount. It has no Print and no Return action.

The permission dialog lists Counter before Management. Sales follows Billing and has Read and Return. The keys are `sales:read` and `sales:return`. The access count is `n / 35`.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner sees Billing, then Sales, under Counter. A staff member with `sales:read` sees Sales. A staff member with only `sales:return` who opens `/sales` lands on the dashboard.
- The page shows the three cards. An invoice row opens the invoice paper. Return is absent without `sales:return`. A return row opens the summary and does not show Print.
- The Manage Users dialog includes Sales Read and Sales Return. The access count uses 35.
