# Plans

When a master user approves the pharmacy, the pharmacy is placed on Essential. Essential is stored as Free. See [Pharmacy signup](pharmacy-signup.md).

## Catalog

| Plan | Stored as | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Annual bill | Included seats | What the pharmacy app says |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Free | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 | 2 | Billing / POS, GST invoices, inventory, batches, expiry, purchases. No card, no expiry. |
| Care | Starter | ₹999 | ₹899 (10% off) | ₹10,788 | 2 | Everything in Essential, all paid screens, email support. |
| Growth | Growth | ₹1,499 | ₹1,299 (13% off) | ₹15,588 | 5 | Everything in Care, all paid screens, up to 5 users. Marked “Popular”. |
| Flagship | Pro | ₹2,499 | ₹2,199 (12% off) | ₹26,388 | Unlimited | Everything in Growth, all paid screens, unlimited users and branches, priority support. |

The Flagship line “unlimited users and branches” is the plan text. Branch rules are documented later.

## Seats

The owner counts as one of the included seats.

| Plan | Other users the owner can create |
| --- | --- |
| Essential | 1 |
| Care | 1 |
| Growth | 4 |
| Flagship | No seat limit |

The app blocks creation past the limit. See [Pharmacy staff login](pharmacy-staff-login.md).

## Upgrade

Only the pharmacy owner can request an upgrade.

The owner chooses Care, Growth, or Flagship, and chooses monthly or annual.

The request goes to an admin user with the support role. See [Admin login](admin-login.md).

The pharmacy stays on its current plan until support approves the request.

A permission key does not open a screen the plan does not include. The paywall stays in front of that screen. See [Permissions](../access/permissions.md).

Support may approve the request, or reject it with comments. The owner sees the result only. The owner does not see support’s comments.

Payment collection and downgrades are documented later.
